How long can an animation be?

The whole animation lives in the device's RAM, so length is capped by free memory — roughly 24 s at 10 fps on a fresh device. The upload panel shows the real limit once a video is selected.

Image refresh on the wheel is rpm ÷ 10 — 20 fps at 200 rpm. Below ~100 rpm video starts to look choppy.

Why do the LEDs stay dark while I pedal slowly?

Below 100 rpm the wheel turns too slowly for the image to hold together, so the arms are powered down. They light up again above 120 rpm. After 3 s without rotation the device idles, and after a minute it sleeps until it feels a bump.

What do these two numbers mean?

Angular step is how far an arm travels between two LED updates — the real limit of sharpness at the rim. It grows with speed: about 1° at 190 rpm, 2° at 390 rpm.

Frame build time must stay well under the time it takes to clock a frame out (~860 µs), otherwise the processor, not the LED bus, becomes the bottleneck.

Colour

Gamma, saturation and contrast shape the picture; R/G/B set the white balance of the LEDs.

Gamma 2.5

Higher = deeper shadows and a cleaner black; lower = flatter, brighter mid-tones.
