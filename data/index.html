Colour

Gamma, saturation and contrast shape the picture; R/G/B set the white balance of the LEDs.

Gamma 2.5

Higher = deeper shadows and a cleaner black; lower = flatter, brighter mid-tones.

Saturation 1.5

How vivid the colours are. 1.0 is the picture as uploaded; higher makes colours purer and further apart from grey.

Contrast 5%

Pushes light areas lighter and dark areas darker. A few percent is usually enough — large values swallow detail at both ends.

Start / stop speed

Below the stop threshold the wheel turns too slowly for the image to hold together, so the arms are powered down. The two values must differ — without that gap the picture would flicker on and off at the boundary.
